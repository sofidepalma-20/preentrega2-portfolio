About

I’m Sofía De Palma, a Visual Design and Communication graduate. I’m passionate about building brands from the inside out — starting from their purpose and values, and shaping how they express themselves visually and digitally. My approach focuses on creating design systems that are solid, clear, and expressive, where every decision has intention.

Since 2021, I’ve worked on branding, editorial design, and web design with a UX/UI focus, supporting entrepreneurs, cultural institutions, and organizations in defining their identity and the way they communicate with their audiences. In the digital field, I design user-centered interfaces and experiences, prioritizing accessibility, visual coherence, and clarity in communication.

I’m also interested in the relationship between design and education, which is why I work as a university instructor.

I believe design is a space to listen, interpret, and transform ideas into meaningful visual experiences.

Experience

Co-Founder & Designer — SoJo Studio (Present)

As co-founder of SoJo Studio, I develop visual identities for personal brands and small businesses. My work involves creative direction, project management, and direct communication with clients to ensure coherent and impactful visual narratives that strengthen each brand’s identity.

Editorial Designer — ABREMATE (2024–2025)

At ABREMATE, I worked on the editorial design and layout of scientific publications, manuals, and educational materials. I adapted content to multiple formats—print, interactive PDFs, and e-books—collaborating with interdisciplinary teams to maintain visual and communicational consistency across all outputs.

Instructor — Universidad Nacional de Lanús (2021–2024)

As a university instructor, I guided and supported students in the development of editorial and design projects within the courses Espacio Tipográfico I (Corvalán), Espacio Tipográfico III (Ibarra / De Losa), and Taller de Diseño IV (Mandel / Sanchez), fostering both conceptual thinking and technical execution in design.

Brand Designer & Web Designer — Munda (2021–2024)

At Munda, I was responsible for brand design and the development of visual systems, from research and strategic planning to implementation. I also designed responsive and accessible web interfaces, creating design systems and prototypes in close collaboration with development teams to ensure visual consistency and usability.
